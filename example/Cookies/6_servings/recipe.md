# Choc Drop Cookies 1/2 c. butter 1 c. br. sugar 1 1/2 c. sour milk 1/2 c. Cake Flour (Co...

- **Servings:** 6 (scaled from 2)
- **Scale Factor:** 3.0x
- **Dietary Mode:** Original

## Ingredients

- 1.5 c. **butter**
- 3 c. **brown sugar**
- 6 **egg** *(well beaten)*
- 1.5 c. **sour milk**
- 6 sq. **chocolate**
- 4.5 c. **Cake Flour** *(Corn meal)*
- 0.75 t. **Soda**
- 0.75 t. **Baking Powder**
- 1.5 t. **Salt**

## Tools & Equipment

- greased Sheet

## Preparation

1. Cream butter and sugar
2. add egg and sour milk then chocolate
3. Quickly fold in sifted drying. and stir only till well blended
4. Drop on greased Sheet
5. Bake 375 10 min
   - *(duration: 10 min; conditions: 375)*
