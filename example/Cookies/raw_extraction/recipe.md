# Choc Drop Cookies 1/2 c. butter 1 c. br. sugar 1 1/2 c. cake flour (ormore) 1/4 t. B.P....

- **Servings:** 2
- **Dietary Mode:** Original

## Ingredients

- 1/2 c. **butter**
- 1 c. **brown sugar**
- 1/2 c. **cake flour**
- 1/4 t. **soda**
- 1/4 t. **baking powder**
- 1/2 t. **salt**
- 2 sq. **chocolate**

## Tools & Equipment

- sheet
- pan

## Preparation

1. Cream butter and sugar, add egg and 1/2 c. sour milk
2. 1/2 c. Cake Flour (Corn M e)
3. 1/4 t. Soda
4. 1/4 t. B.P.
5. 1/2 t. Salt
6. 2 sq. chocolate
7. Cream butter and sugar, add egg and sour milk then chocolate.
8. Quickly fold in sifted drying.
9. and stir only till well blended.
10. Drop on greased Sheet - Bake 375 10 min.
   - *(duration: 10 min.; conditions: 375)*
11. 2 dozen
12. Granama Kohler
