# Choc Drop Cookies 1/2 c. butter 1 c. br. sugar 1 1/2 c. sour milk 1/2 c. Cake Flour (Co...

- **Servings:** 2
- **Dietary Mode:** Veganized

## Ingredients

- 1/2 c. **vegan butter** *(vegan substitute for: 1/2 c. butter)*
- 1 c. **brown sugar**
- 2 **vegan egg** *(well beaten; vegan substitute for: 2 egg)*
- 1/2 c. **sour milk**
- 2 sq. **chocolate**
- 1 1/2 c. **Cake Flour** *(Corn meal)*
- 1/4 t. **Soda**
- 1/4 t. **Baking Powder**
- 1/2 t. **Salt**
- sifted **drying**

## Tools & Equipment

- Sheet

## Preparation

1. Cream vegan butter and sugar
2. Add vegan egg and sour milk then chocolate
3. Quickly fold in sifted drying. and stir only till well blended
4. Drop on greased Sheet
5. Bake 375 10 min
   - *(duration: 10 min; conditions: 375)*
